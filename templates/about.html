DATA SCIENCE PROJECT

Customer behaviour → store location.

SmartStore AI answers a practical retail question: if a company wants to sell a particular category, which Bengaluru area already shows the strongest demand?

Inputs

Historical orders, customers, average spending, purchase quantity, repeat rate, digital payment activity and revenue.

ML pipeline

Pandas data preparation → feature engineering → Random Forest classification → Joblib .pkl model → Flask prediction interface.

Business output

Area ranking, demand score, success probability, estimated monthly orders, revenue and profit.

Local-first

The complete application runs on your laptop with Flask and SQLite. No external server is required.
